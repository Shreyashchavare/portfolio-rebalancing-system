## Scope and Objectives

## Asset Allocation Limits
1. Total equity asset class allocation must not exceed 60% of total portfolio value.
2. Single stock exposure for any individual instrument must not exceed 8% of portfolio market value.
3. Emerging markets equities are capped at a maximum of 15% of total equity holdings.
